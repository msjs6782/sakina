Natural Earth (map data for the offline qibla map)
==================================================
Natural Earth Vector v5.1.2 — 1:110m land and lakes, 1:50m land and land boundary lines.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com — https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector

"All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public
domain. You may use the maps in any manner, including modifying the content and design, electronic
dissemination, and offset printing. The primary authors, Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso,
and all other contributors renounce all financial claim to the maps and invites you to use them for
personal, educational, and commercial purposes. No permission is needed to use Natural Earth.
Crediting the authors is unnecessary."

Sakina stores the coordinates rounded to about 0.6 km. Boundaries are shown as Natural Earth draws
them; they are not a statement about any territory.


World Magnetic Model WMM2025 (true-north correction of the compass)
===================================================================
WMM2025 coefficients (WMM.COF, epoch 2025.0, released 13 November 2024), valid from 2025.0 to 2030.0.
Produced by the U.S. National Centers for Environmental Information (NOAA NCEI) and the British
Geological Survey for the U.S. National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency and the UK Defence Geographic
Centre. https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model

The WMM is a product of the U.S. federal government and is not subject to copyright protection in
the United States. Sakina evaluates the model on the device; its tests check the results against
NOAA's 100 official WMM2025 test values.
