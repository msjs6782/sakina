Adhkar texts and grades
=======================
Hadith texts (Arabic, with vowel marks), sunnah.com-style numbering and graders' rulings for the adhkar library
come from fawazahmed0/hadith-api (https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api), editions ara-* and eng-*,
fetched through jsDelivr (@1) in October 2026; repository head at the time: df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784 (2026-06-03).
Each dhikr is the wording of the cited narration, located by phrase and checked by tools/adhkar/build.py;
the exact hadith texts used are kept in tools/adhkar/sources.json. Ayah texts come from the bundled Tanzil
Uthmani text. Links point to sunnah.com and dorar.net for checking.
